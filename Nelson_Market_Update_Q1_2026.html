What this means for buyers

Under $600K: move quickly — demand is high and supply is thin. Between $600K–$800K: you have options and real negotiating room. Over $1M: 22 listings, only 2 sold — you are in the driver's seat.

What this means for sellers

The $600K–$800K band has a 30% sell-through rate — 7 out of 10 homes are sitting. The ones that sold were priced correctly from day one. Price it right or watch it expire.
