About this report

Every quarter I pull the raw MLS data for Nelson and the surrounding West Kootenay areas and publish what it actually says — including the parts that aren't flattering to my own industry.

Two rules run through every edition. Medians, not averages, because a handful of outliers can move an average and tell you a story that isn't true. And always two numbers: the all-types median, and the Nelson-proper detached median — because the first one swings with the product mix while the second is the honest read on what homes are worth.

Coverage: Nelson (NE) plus the NERU sub-areas — North Nelson to Kokanee Creek, Nelson West/South Slocan, and Nelson South/Salmo Rural. Data compiled from the Association of Interior REALTORS®.
